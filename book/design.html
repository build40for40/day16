Roast celeriac with brown butter and hazelnuts
Serves four · 50 minutes
  • 1 large celeriac 18 mi
  • 60 g salted butter 31 mi
  • A handful of sage 0 mi
  • 50 g hazelnuts 96 mi
  • 1 tbsp cider vinegar 40 mi

Scrub the celeriac and cut it into thick wedges. Roast at 200°C until the edges catch and a knife slides in, about forty minutes. Melt the butter until it smells of toast, drop in the sage and let it crisp. Spoon over the celeriac, scatter the chopped hazelnuts, and finish with the vinegar.

Specification

How it is made

Illustration

A hand-drawn, engraved spiral in ink line, filled in the colours of the spice rack: cinnamon at the centre, then paprika, saffron and turmeric at the rim.

Format

A5 (148 × 210 mm) hardback, one recipe per page, laid out by the page builder in the repo. Printed on demand by Lulu. ISBN 978-1-291-41537-7.

Typography

Cormorant Garamond for titles and small capitals, EB Garamond for the text. Black on warm uncoated paper, one spot colour for numerals and labels.

Binding

Cloth over board, foil stamped spine and front, ribbon marker, headband, sewn signatures so it lies open on the counter.

Structure

Twelve months, each opening with a short note on what is in the ground. Recipes run January to December, with the receipt of miles under every one.

Recommendation

Go with B

The ivory and oxblood cover is the most minimal of the three. It shares its palette with the interior, where oxblood is the single accent and the copyright page stays black. A is the fallback for a richer cloth edition, and C works well as the website header and social image.
