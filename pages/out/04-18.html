Lentil, radish and orange salad

a saucepan, a frying pan, a sieve, a large bowl
45m: 20m prep + 25m cook
ingredients
200g puy lentils
3 tbsp olive oil
1 tbsp red wine vinegar
1 tsp Dijon mustard
40g pumpkin seeds
3 oranges
200g radishes
4 spring onions
100g watercress
seasoning
bay leaves
parsley
salt
black pepper
method
  1. 1Simmer the lentils and bay leaves in plenty of water for 20 to 25 minutes, until just tender. Drain.
  2. 2Whisk the oil, vinegar and mustard, then toss through the warm lentils.
  3. 3Toast the pumpkin seeds in a dry pan until they pop.
  4. 4Slice the peel off the oranges and cut into rounds. Thinly slice the radishes and spring onions.
  5. 5Toss everything with the watercress and chopped parsley. Season and scatter with the seeds.
