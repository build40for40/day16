Apple and seed flapjacks

one large pan, a 20cm square baking tin, a grater
50m: 15m prep + 35m cook
ingredients
150g butter
60g soft brown sugar
3 tbsp golden syrup
300g porridge oats
2 eating apples
40g sunflower seeds
50g sultanas
seasoning
cinnamon
salt
method
  1. 1Heat the oven to 180°C (160°C fan) and butter a 20cm square tin.
  2. 2Melt the butter, sugar and syrup in a large pan over a low heat.
  3. 3Off the heat, stir in the oats, grated apples with their skins, seeds, sultanas, 1 tsp cinnamon and a pinch of salt.
  4. 4Press firmly into the tin. Bake for 30 to 35 minutes, until golden at the edges.
  5. 5Cool in the tin, then cut into 16 bars.
