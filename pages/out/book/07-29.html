Cavolo nero and kipper fritters

two bowls, a large frying pan, a whisk
35m: 30m prep + 5m cook
ingredients
4 kipper fillets
200g cavolo nero
3 tbsp sunflower oil
150g self-raising flour
2 eggs
150ml milk
4 spring onions
200g natural yoghurt
seasoning
black pepper
dill
lemon
method
  1. 1Cover the kippers with boiling water for 5 minutes. Drain and flake, losing the skin and bones.
  2. 2Strip and shred the cavolo nero leaves and wilt in the frying pan with a little oil.
  3. 3Whisk the flour, eggs and milk to a thick batter. Stir in the greens, kipper, sliced spring onions and pepper.
  4. 4Fry spoonfuls in hot oil for 2 to 3 minutes a side, until golden.
  5. 5Mix the yoghurt with chopped dill and lemon. Serve alongside.
