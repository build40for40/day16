Cavolo nero and bacon stuffed jackets

a roasting tray, a large frying pan, a bowl
115m: 25m prep + 90m cook
ingredients
4 large baking potatoes
200g smoked streaky bacon
30g butter
2 cloves garlic
400g cavolo nero
1/4 red cabbage
2 tbsp cider vinegar
100ml crème fraîche
80g cheddar
seasoning
salt
black pepper
method
  1. 1Prick the potatoes and bake at 200°C (180°C fan) for 1¼ hours.
  2. 2Fry the chopped bacon until crisp. Add the butter, sliced garlic and shredded cavolo nero. Wilt for 5 minutes.
  3. 3Toss the shredded red cabbage with the vinegar and salt to serve alongside.
  4. 4Halve the potatoes. Mash the flesh with the crème fraîche, bacon mix and half the cheese, then season. Refill, top with the rest and bake for 15 minutes.
