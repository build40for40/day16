Asparagus, broad bean and pork frittata

a large ovenproof frying pan, a whisk
45m: 30m prep + 15m cook
ingredients
1 tbsp olive oil
400g new potatoes
250g pork mince
500g broad beans in pods
250g asparagus
8 eggs
40g Parmesan, grated
100g rocket
seasoning
salt
black pepper
fennel seeds
chives
method
  1. 1Heat the oven to 200°C (180°C fan). Fry the thinly sliced potatoes in the oil for 10 minutes, until golden.
  2. 2Add the pork, 1 tsp fennel seeds and a pinch of salt. Fry until browned.
  3. 3Add the podded beans, the asparagus in short lengths and a splash of water. Cover for 3 minutes.
  4. 4Whisk the eggs with the Parmesan, snipped chives and seasoning. Pour in and bake for 15 minutes, until just set. Serve with rocket.
