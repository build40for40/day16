Roast beetroot and fennel pasta with goat's cheese

a roasting tray, a large pan, a sieve
60m: 25m prep + 35m cook
ingredients
4 beetroot
2 fennel bulbs
1 red onion
3 tbsp olive oil
1 tin chickpeas
50g walnuts
350g penne
100g goat's cheese
seasoning
salt
black pepper
fennel seeds
lemon
dill
method
  1. 1Heat the oven to 200°C (180°C fan). Cut the peeled beetroot, the fennel and the onion into wedges, keeping the fennel fronds.
  2. 2Toss on a tray with the oil, drained chickpeas, 1 tsp fennel seeds and seasoning. Roast for 35 minutes, adding the walnuts for the last 5.
  3. 3Cook the pasta, then drain, saving a cupful of the water.
  4. 4Toss the pasta into the tray with lemon zest, juice and a splash of the water. Crumble over the goat's cheese, dill and fronds.
