Marrow and peach on toast with goat's cheese

a large frying pan, a toaster or grill
25m: 15m prep + 10m cook
ingredients
1 small marrow
3 tbsp olive oil
2 cloves garlic
4 thick slices sourdough
150g soft goat's cheese
2 ripe peaches
seasoning
salt
black pepper
chilli flakes
lemon
mint
method
  1. 1Peel the marrow, scoop out the seeds and cut into small cubes. Fry in 2 tbsp of the oil over a high heat for 8 to 10 minutes, until golden and tender.
  2. 2Stir in the sliced garlic and a pinch of chilli flakes for 1 minute. Season and add a squeeze of lemon.
  3. 3Toast the bread and spread thickly with the goat's cheese.
  4. 4Pile on the marrow, then the sliced peaches and torn mint. Finish with the rest of the oil.
