Spinach and feta dip with crunchy veg

a saucepan, a sieve, a grater, a mixing bowl
20m: 15m prep + 5m cook
ingredients
200g spinach
1 garlic clove
250g Greek yoghurt
100g feta
1 tbsp olive oil
3 carrots
1 cucumber
1 bunch radishes
seasoning
dill
lemon
black pepper
method
  1. 1Wilt the spinach in a pan with a splash of water for 2 minutes. Cool in a sieve, squeeze out the water and chop finely.
  2. 2Mix with the grated garlic, yoghurt, crumbled feta, chopped dill and the lemon zest and juice. Season with pepper.
  3. 3Spoon into a bowl and drizzle with the oil.
  4. 4Cut the carrots and cucumber into sticks and halve the radishes for dipping.
