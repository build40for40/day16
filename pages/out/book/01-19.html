Baked pears with walnuts and honey

a baking dish, a small bowl
40m: 10m prep + 30m cook
ingredients
4 pears
50g walnuts
2 tbsp honey
25g soft butter
1 orange
200g Greek yoghurt
seasoning
cinnamon
method
  1. 1Heat the oven to 190°C (170°C fan). Halve the pears, scoop out the cores and sit them cut side up in a baking dish.
  2. 2Mix the chopped walnuts, honey, butter, orange zest and a good pinch of cinnamon. Spoon into the hollows.
  3. 3Pour the orange juice and 3 tbsp water around the pears. Bake for 25 to 30 minutes, until a knife slides in easily.
  4. 4Serve warm with the yoghurt and a spoonful of the pan juices.
