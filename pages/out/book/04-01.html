Leek and egg pie with a new potato top

a large pan, a saucepan, a baking dish
55m: 30m prep + 25m cook
ingredients
800g new potatoes
6 eggs
3 leeks
30g butter
2 tbsp cornflour
500ml milk
200g spinach
100g grated Cheddar
seasoning
salt
black pepper
nutmeg
chives
method
  1. 1Heat the oven to 200°C (180°C fan). Boil the potatoes and eggs for 8 minutes, then cool the eggs.
  2. 2Soften the sliced leeks in the butter for 8 minutes. Stir in the cornflour, then the milk, until thick.
  3. 3Wilt in the spinach, half the cheese, a grating of nutmeg and the chopped chives. Season and tip into a baking dish.
  4. 4Tuck in the peeled, halved eggs. Top with the sliced potatoes and the rest of the cheese. Bake for 25 minutes, until golden.
