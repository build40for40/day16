Roast rhubarb and ricotta on toast

a baking dish, a frying pan, a toaster
25m: 10m prep + 15m cook
ingredients
400g rhubarb
1 orange
2 tbsp honey
50g hazelnuts
8 slices wholemeal bread
250g ricotta
seasoning
cinnamon
method
  1. 1Heat the oven to 200°C (180°C fan). Lay the rhubarb, cut into 4cm lengths, in a baking dish.
  2. 2Add the orange zest and juice, the honey and a pinch of cinnamon. Roast for 12 to 15 minutes, until tender but holding its shape.
  3. 3Toast the hazelnuts in a dry pan until golden, then chop roughly.
  4. 4Toast the bread, spread thickly with ricotta and spoon over the rhubarb and its juices. Scatter with the nuts.
