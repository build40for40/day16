Grape and plum oat crumble

a baking dish, a mixing bowl
42m: 12m prep + 30m cook
ingredients
400g red grapes
4 plums
1 tsp cornflour
100g porridge oats
50g plain flour
40g almonds
2 tbsp soft brown sugar
4 tbsp rapeseed oil
seasoning
cinnamon
method
  1. 1Heat the oven to 200°C (180°C fan). Stone and chop the plums.
  2. 2Toss the plums and grapes with the cornflour and half a tsp of cinnamon in a baking dish.
  3. 3Mix the oats, flour, chopped almonds, sugar and the rest of a tsp of cinnamon. Stir in the oil until it clumps.
  4. 4Scatter over the fruit. Bake for 25 to 30 minutes, until golden and bubbling at the edges. Cool a little before eating.
