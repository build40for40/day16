Orange and rhubarb compote with yoghurt

a saucepan, a frying pan
23m: 15m prep + 8m cook
ingredients
4 oranges
400g rhubarb
2 tbsp honey
50g porridge oats
30g almonds
500g natural yoghurt
seasoning
cinnamon
method
  1. 1Grate the zest of one orange. Cut the peel off all four, slice into rounds and save the juice.
  2. 2Simmer the rhubarb, cut into short lengths, with the juice, zest, honey and a pinch of cinnamon for 5 minutes, until just soft.
  3. 3Take off the heat and stir in the orange slices.
  4. 4Toast the oats and chopped almonds in a dry pan until golden. Spoon the compote over the yoghurt and scatter with the crunch.
