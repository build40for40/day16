- 14 January, Chicken, mushroom and butter bean stew: **honest_times**. The step timings add up to 31 minutes on the hob against 25 printed, and 15 minutes of prep cannot cover chopping 8 thighs, 2 leeks, 400g mushrooms, garlic and kale plus 11 minutes of hands-on browning and frying.
- 18 February, Potato, mushroom and kale tray with baked eggs: **honest_times**. A quick breakfast was asked for, but the 25 + 10 + 8 minutes in the oven plus 12 minutes' prep comes to nearly an hour, which no busy parent would call quick in the morning.
- 4 April, Salad leaf bowls with crispy pork and apple: **works**. Step 4 tosses 250g of delicate salad leaves straight into a tray just out of a 220°C oven with hot oily juices, so the hero leaves will collapse into a soggy wilted mass. They should be dressed in the bowls, or the tray left to cool first.
- 19 April, Lamb chops on creamy spinach and butter beans: **works**. The chops roast for 23 minutes in total at 220°C, which would leave ordinary lamb chops dry and grey, and the Bramley wedges would collapse into sour mush through the creamy beans; stirring 600g of raw spinach on a tray outside the oven 'until it wilts' would also be slow and messy.
- 9 May, Roast rhubarb with gammon and eggs: **works**. Eggs cracked into gaps on a flat tray awash with a whole orange's juice and rhubarb liquid will run into the syrup rather than set neatly, and 4cm rhubarb roasted for 20 to 22 minutes at 220°C will collapse to mush and may catch with the honey.
- 5 June, Whiting on tomato and butter beans: **supermarket**. Whiting fillets are rarely stocked at mainstream supermarkets like Tesco and usually need a fishmonger, so the reader may not find them; suggest an alternative such as pollock or cod.
- 10 June, Roast cherry tomato dip with trout: **works**. Blending 500g of juicy roasted cherry tomatoes, with no draining or reducing, into only 150g yoghurt (still warm, so it may split) gives a thin sauce rather than a dip that will cling to veg sticks.
- 7 September, Warm blackberries on toast with kipper ricotta: **works**. Piling honey-sweetened, jammy blackberries straight onto smoked kipper ricotta is a jarring sweet and smoky-fish clash most families would not enjoy at breakfast, so it is unlikely to come out as a good dish.
- 8 October, Pumpkin risotto with whiting and sprouts: **supermarket**. Whiting fillets are rarely on the shelf or fish counter at a mainstream supermarket like Tesco, so most readers would need a fishmonger or a swap such as pollock or cod.
- 8 October, Pumpkin risotto with whiting and sprouts: **honest_times**. The steps describe 5 minutes softening, 18 minutes of ladling and stirring and 5 minutes covered, so the cooking runs to about 28 mostly active minutes, not the 15 printed, and the whole meal takes well past a quick dinner.
- 20 October, Leek and sweetcorn fritters with tomatoes: **honest_times**. The 10 cook minutes cannot cover the 8 minutes softening the leeks, the fritters fried at 3 minutes a side (which for four means two or three batches, roughly 15 to 18 minutes at the stove) and the 3 minutes for the tomatoes, so the real hands-on time runs well past the 40 minutes printed in total.
- 21 October, Cider mussels and cabbage on toast: **works**. The 200ml of cider plus the mussel liquor is never reduced before the crème fraîche goes in, so roughly 400ml of thin liquid gets piled onto four slices of toast and turns them soggy.
- 2 November, Squash and red lentil soup with kale: **honest_times**. The 15 minutes of prep has to cover peeling and cubing 1kg of butternut squash, chopping the onion and garlic, shredding the kale, toasting the seeds and softening the onion for 5 minutes, which realistically runs nearer 25, so 40 minutes in all is slow for a quick dinner.
- 16 November, Roast celeriac and chickpeas with tahini: **honest_ingredients**. The seasoning list gives smoked paprika but step 1 says '1 tsp paprika', which is a separate cupboard item, so a reader with both may reach for the wrong one.
- 11 December, Ham, cheddar and Bramley breakfast loaf: **honest_times**. The printed 60 cook minutes do not match the 50-minute bake in the steps, and the time_note is blank, so nothing tells a reader that a breakfast needing 70-plus minutes and cooling before slicing must be made ahead.
- 20 December, Pork chop, artichoke and apple bake: **honest_times**. The 50-minute cook time makes this a 65-minute dinner, well past the quick pace asked for.
