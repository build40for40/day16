Celeriac and apple soup with rye crumbs

one heavy pan, a blender, one frying pan
40m: 5m prep + 35m cook
ingredients
1 large celeriac
2 leeks
2 Cox apples
250ml whole milk
2 slices rye bread
2 tbsp rapeseed oil
seasoning
salt
thyme
method
  1. 1Warm the oil in a heavy pan. Soften the sliced leeks with a pinch of salt for 8 minutes.
  2. 2Add the cubed celeriac and apples, the thyme and 800 ml water. Simmer for 25 minutes, until the celeriac is soft.
  3. 3Blend with the milk until smooth. Season.
  4. 4Tear the rye into crumbs, toast in a dry pan until crisp, and scatter over each bowl.
