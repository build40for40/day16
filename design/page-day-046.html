Delicata squash and white bean stew with kale

one pot, one roasting tray, one frying pan
75m: 10m prep + 65m cook, beans soaked overnight
ingredients
2 delicata squash
1 bunch kale
1 cup dried white beans
1 yellow onion
4 garlic cloves
1 tbsp maple syrup
2 tbsp butter
method
  1. 1Simmer the soaked beans with half the onion until tender, about an hour.
  2. 2Slice the squash into half moons. Roast with butter and maple at 425°F / 220°C for 25 minutes.
  3. 3Cook the garlic, sage and remaining onion in butter. Add the kale and stir until bright, about 5 minutes.
  4. 4Fold the squash and beans into the kale with a ladle of bean broth. Season.
