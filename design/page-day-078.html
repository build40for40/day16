Porridge with apple and rhubarb compote

small frying pan, 2 saucepans, one with a lid, grater
20m: 15m prep + 5m cook
ingredients
40g pumpkin seeds
4 eating apples, cored and chopped, skin on
200g rhubarb, cut into 2cm pieces
1 orange, zested and juiced
1 tbsp soft brown sugar
200g porridge oats
seasoning
1 tsp cinnamon
1.2 litres plus 3 tbsp water
a pinch salt
method
  1. 1Toast the pumpkin seeds in a dry frying pan over a medium heat for 3 to 4 minutes, shaking the pan, until they start to pop and smell nutty. Tip onto a plate.
  2. 2Put the apples, rhubarb, orange zest and juice, cinnamon, sugar and 3 tbsp water in a saucepan. Stir, cover and bring to a gentle simmer.
  3. 3Cook for 8 to 10 minutes, stirring now and then, until the apples are tender and the rhubarb has collapsed into a soft pink sauce.
  4. 4Meanwhile, put the oats, 1.2 litres water and a pinch of salt in another pan. Bring to the boil, then simmer for 5 to 6 minutes, stirring often, until thick and creamy.
  5. 5Spoon the porridge into bowls, top with the warm compote and scatter over the toasted seeds.
