Parsnip and red lentil dal with spinach

large saucepan with a lid, ladle
40m: 15m prep + 25m cook
ingredients
2 tbsp rapeseed oil
1 large onion, chopped
500g parsnips, scrubbed, trimmed and cut into 2cm chunks
3 cloves garlic, crushed
250g red lentils, rinsed
1.2 litres vegetable stock
150g spinach
1 lemon, juiced
4 tbsp natural yoghurt
seasoning
1 tsp salt
1 tbsp mild curry powder
black pepper, to taste
method
  1. 1Heat the oil in a large saucepan over a medium heat. Add the onion with a pinch of the salt and cook for 8 minutes, stirring now and then, until soft and turning golden. Chop the parsnips while it cooks.
  2. 2Stir in the garlic and curry powder and cook for 1 minute, until it smells fragrant.
  3. 3Add the parsnips, lentils and stock and bring to the boil. Cover and simmer for 20 to 25 minutes, stirring now and then so nothing sticks, until the lentils have collapsed and the parsnips are tender.
  4. 4Stir in the spinach a handful at a time until it wilts, 1 to 2 minutes. Add a splash of water if the dal is thicker than you like.
  5. 5Squeeze in the lemon juice, then season with the rest of the salt and plenty of black pepper. Ladle into bowls and top each with a spoonful of yoghurt.
