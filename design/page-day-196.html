Smashed broad beans, peas and ricotta on sourdough

one saucepan, one bowl, a toaster
25m: 20m prep + 5m cook
ingredients
500g broad beans in pods
250g peas in pods
150g fresh ricotta
4 slices sourdough
3 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
seasoning
salt
mint
lemon
method
  1. 1Pod the broad beans and peas. Blanch the beans for 2 minutes, add the peas for the last 30 seconds, then cool in cold water.
  2. 2Slip the broad beans out of their grey skins.
  3. 3Crush beans and peas roughly with oil, lemon zest and juice, torn mint and salt.
  4. 4Toast the sourdough. Spread with ricotta, pile on the smash and finish with oil.
