Raspberry and peach oat crumble

baking dish, about 20 x 25cm, mixing bowl, scales, measuring spoons
55m: 15m prep + 40m cook
ingredients
4 ripe peaches or nectarines, stoned and sliced
300g raspberries
½ lemon, juiced
1 tbsp caster sugar
1 tsp cornflour
75g plain flour
75g porridge oats
40g almonds, roughly chopped
50g soft brown sugar
5 tbsp sunflower oil
seasoning
1 tsp cinnamon
a pinch salt
method
  1. 1Heat the oven to 180°C fan, 200°C conventional.
  2. 2Put the peach slices and raspberries in the baking dish. Add the lemon juice, caster sugar and cornflour, then toss gently so the raspberries mostly stay whole.
  3. 3In a bowl, mix the flour, oats, almonds, brown sugar, cinnamon and salt. Pour in the oil and stir with a fork until it forms damp, crumbly clumps.
  4. 4Scatter the topping evenly over the fruit, leaving a few gaps so the juices can bubble through.
  5. 5Bake for 30 to 35 minutes, until the top is golden and crisp and the fruit is bubbling thickly at the edges.
  6. 6Leave to cool for 10 minutes so the juices settle and thicken, then spoon into bowls.
