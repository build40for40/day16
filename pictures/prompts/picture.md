## What to draw

- The dish as it looks at the end of the last step, in the pan, tray or bowl a home cook would bring to the table. One dish, not four portions, unless the recipe makes separate pieces such as scones or fritters.
- Show each ingredient the way the method leaves it: grated, wilted, roasted, blended. An ingredient that has dissolved or been blended away is not drawn.
- Beside the dish, one or two of the fresh ingredients raw and whole, as a small still life. Choose the ones named in the title.
- Nothing edible that is not in the ingredients or seasoning lists. No garnish, herb sprig, lemon wedge, bread or side dish unless it is listed.
- Plain crockery and an ordinary pan. No hands, people, cutlery, napkins, table or background scene.

## Style

{{STYLE}}
- Hand drawn, not photographic, not a 3D render, not glossy, not a cartoon, not a digital vector.
- No text, letters, numbers, labels, captions, arrows, signature or watermark anywhere.

## Composition

Landscape, 16:9. The page crops the picture to a wide strip about twice as wide as it is tall, so keep everything inside the middle 80% of the height, with the dish just left of centre and the raw ingredients to its right. Leave generous white space around the group.
